A play designer that tests, not just draws

Most flag football play makers are drawing tools. They’re good at routes, play cards and wristbands, and some animate the routes as drawn. But the defense on the page never moves, so the drawing can’t tell you whether the play will work.

Open Grass starts where they stop. Zone defenders guard their areas, man defenders chase their receivers, a blitzer can rush the quarterback, and a heatmap shows where the open grass is while the play runs. You see why a route is open, not just that it is.

  • A–F grade against every coverage in one scouting report, so you know which looks a play beats.
  • QB read progression for each coverage: who’s open, who’s tight, who’s covered, and when.
  • Coach’s adjustments: route changes that raise the grade, each with a one-tap Try it.
  • Runs too: handoffs, pitches and trick plays are graded with pursuit.

Example: Twins Right, Corner-Flat

Two receivers to the right run a corner and a flat. One defender can’t cover both, so against zone the quarterback reads that defender and throws to whichever receiver that defender leaves. On the backside, a slant gives the quarterback a quick answer.

Man coverage takes it away, because every receiver has a defender running with them. Open Grass suggests turning the corner route into a slant so the two routes cross and the man defenders have to fight through traffic. That one change raises the grade against man from a D to a B.

Questions

How do I create a play?

Tap New play. Describe the play in your own words, or upload a photo or screenshot of a play card or whiteboard, and Open Grass draws it as a draft you can test and save. You can also import plays from an Open Grass playbook file.

Is the play designer free?

Yes, while Open Grass is in beta. A free account saves your plays, favorites and share links. Early coaches get founding-coach perks.

Which defenses can I test against?

Cover 1, Cover 2, Cover 3 and man to man, each with an optional linebacker or safety blitz. If the other team lines up differently, drag any defender to match what you see.

Does it work for 7-on-7?

Open Grass is built for 5-on-5 flag football today. More formats are on the way.

Are my plays private?

Yes. Plays you save stay in your browser, or in your private account when you’re signed in. A play is shared only when you send someone a share link. See the privacy policy for the details.
