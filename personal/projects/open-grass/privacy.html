Privacy Policy

Effective October 7, 2026 · Open Grass, LLC

Open Grass is a play-testing tool for youth flag football coaches at open-grass.com. This policy explains what information we collect, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the choices you have. "We" and "us" mean Open Grass, LLC.

The short version

  • You can use Open Grass without an account. Your plays then stay in your own browser. You get one free AI draft; after that, AI drafting needs a free account.
  • If you sign in, we store your email (through our sign-in provider), your plays, favorites, and any play-card photos you upload, so they follow you across devices.
  • We use Google Analytics to see which features coaches use. It never receives your plays, play names, descriptions, or email. You can turn it off.
  • We advertise Open Grass on Google and measure whether those ads lead to sign-ups. We don't sell your information, and we don't use it to target ads at you: no ad cookies, no retargeting, no personalized ads.
  • You can download or delete your account and its data at any time from Your data at the bottom of the app.

1. Information we collect

If you use Open Grass without an account

Plays you save, your favorites, and the last play you opened are kept in your browser's local storage on your device. We don't receive them. Clearing your browser data, or using Your data → Clear Open Grass data from this browser, deletes them.

If you create an account

  • Account details. Sign-in is handled by our provider, Clerk. Clerk stores your email address and, if you sign in with Google, the name and profile picture Google shares. Clerk also keeps sign-in records (such as time, IP address and browser) to keep accounts secure. We receive a random account ID from Clerk, not your password.
  • Your plays and favorites. The plays you save, their names, and your list of favorites, stored with your account ID.
  • Play-card photos. If you create a play from a photo or screenshot while signed in, we keep the image so you can view the original later. Only you can access it. If no saved play uses a photo, we delete it after 7 days.

When you use AI drafting

Without an account you get one free AI draft; after that, AI drafting needs a free account. When you describe a play in words or upload a diagram, that text or image is sent to our server and on to Anthropic, which provides the AI model that draws the play. For each request we also record the time, whether it was text or an image, whether it worked, how much AI processing it used, your account ID, and a salted, one-way hash of your IP address. We use these to enforce daily limits, prevent abuse, and manage costs. We do not store your IP address itself. After 90 days we remove the hashed IP and account ID from these records. Daily usage counters are deleted after 30 days.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how the app is used, for example which coverages coaches test, whether AI drafting succeeds, and whether share links get opened. Google Analytics uses cookies (_ga, _ga_*) and collects information such as pages visited, the features you use, your browser and device type, and approximate location (city level, derived from your IP address by Google).

  • We deliberately never send your plays, play names, play descriptions, file names, or email address to Google Analytics, and we remove the part of a link that contains a shared play before the page address is recorded.
  • If you're signed in, we send your random account ID so visits from your different devices count as one coach. We never send your email or name.
  • Google signals and ad personalization are turned off. Analytics data is kept for 14 months.
  • See how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Advertising on Google

We advertise Open Grass with Google Ads. Our Google Ads account is linked to the same Google tag as Analytics, which lets us see whether our ads work:

  • When a page loads, Google Ads receives a signal that the page was viewed, without setting any advertising cookies. Like any web request, it includes your IP address and browser information. It never includes the part of a link that holds a shared play.
  • If you clicked one of our ads and then create an account, Google Ads learns that a sign-up happened, so we can count the sign-ups our ads bring in. It isn't told your email, name, plays, or anything else you put in Open Grass.
  • We don't use remarketing or retargeting (following you around the web with our ads), we don't show personalized ads or build ad audiences, and we don't give Google your email or plays for advertising.
  • Turning off analytics (section 6), or a Global Privacy Control signal from your browser, stops these signals too.

Share links

When you share a play you created or changed, the entire play is encoded inside the link itself. We don't store it on a server. Anyone who has the link can open the play, so share it only with people you want to see it.

Information your browser sends automatically

Like any website, the services that deliver Open Grass receive your IP address and basic browser information when you load the page: our host (GitHub Pages), Google Fonts (for the app's typefaces), and our server provider (Cloudflare) when you use AI drafting or an account.

2. How we use information

  • To run the app: save and sync your plays, draw plays with AI, and show your photos back to you.
  • To keep it working and affordable: rate limits, daily AI limits, abuse prevention, and cost tracking.
  • To improve it: understanding which features coaches use, through analytics.
  • To see whether our ads on Google lead to sign-ups, as described under Advertising on Google.
  • To respond to you when you contact us.

We don't sell personal information, and we don't share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We don't use your plays or photos to train AI models.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Turn off analytics. In the app, open Your data (bottom of the page) and uncheck "Share usage statistics." This also stops the Google Ads signals. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we turn analytics and these ad signals off automatically. You can also use Google's Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
  • Download your data. Signed in, choose Your data → Download my account data for a file with your plays, favorites, photo list and AI draft history. Export favorites also saves your favorite plays as a file you can import elsewhere.
  • Correct your data. Edit or delete plays in the app. Update your email or name from the account menu (your profile picture, top right).
  • Delete your data. Delete any play you've saved, or choose Your data → Delete my account to permanently delete your account, plays, favorites and photos. Not signed in? Use Clear Open Grass data from this browser.
  • Ask us. You can also email us to access, correct or delete your information, including analytics data tied to your account. We'll respond within 30 days and may need to confirm the request comes from you.

Depending on where you live (for example California, or other U.S. states with privacy laws), you may have additional rights, such as to know what we collect, to have it deleted or corrected, and not to be treated differently for using these rights. We honor these rights for all users, wherever they live.

7. Children's privacy

Open Grass is built for coaches, parents and other adults. It isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it. Coaches: please don't put players' full names or other personal details about children into play names, descriptions or photos.

8. Security

Connections to Open Grass are encrypted (HTTPS). Account data is only available to the signed-in account it belongs to, photos are stored privately, and IP addresses are stored only as salted hashes. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify you as required by law if a breach affects it.

9. Where information is processed

Open Grass is operated from the United States. Our providers may process information in the United States and other countries where they operate.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the effective date above. If the changes are significant, we'll also tell you in the app.
