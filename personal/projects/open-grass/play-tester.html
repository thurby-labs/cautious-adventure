Questions

Do I need an account?

Not for your first play. A free account saves your plays and gives you more AI drafts.

Is it a play designer?

No. Keep drawing plays wherever you do now. Open Grass tests the plays you already have and tells you how to make them better.

What if it reads my photo wrong?

Clear diagrams read best: one play per photo, routes drawn as lines with arrows. If a draft comes out wrong, try a sharper photo or describe the play in words.

Are my plays private?

Yes. Your plays stay in your browser, or in your private account when you sign in. A play is shared only when you send a share link. See the privacy policy.
