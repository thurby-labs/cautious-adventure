Test your flag football plays before game day

Open Grass tests the flag football plays you already have. Put a defense on your play, watch it run, and see exactly where the open grass is against Cover 1, Cover 2, Cover 3, Cover 4 and man coverage.

Bring your own playbook

Keep drawing plays wherever you do now. Upload a screenshot from your play app, a photo of your play card or whiteboard, or your whole playbook as a PDF, or just describe the play, and Open Grass puts it on the field. Sample plays are there to try it out.

Watch the defense react

Drag a coverage onto the field or move single defenders to match what the other team runs. Zone defenders guard their areas, man defenders chase their receiver, and a rusher comes after the QB from either side (or plays an extra safety when nobody rushes).

Find the hole

A heatmap shows open grass before the snap and while the play runs, so you can see why a route is open, not just that it is.

Get a grade and a fix

Every play gets an A–F grade against each coverage, a QB read order, and route changes you can try with one tap. Runs are graded too.

Coach questions

Is Open Grass free?

Yes, while it is in beta. A free account saves your plays, favorites and share links. Early coaches get founding-coach perks.

What kind of flag football does it support?

Open Grass handles 5-on-5 and 7-on-7 flag football. A 7-on-7 play gets a 40-yard-wide field and seven defenders, including a rusher from 7 yards.

Which defenses can I test against?

For 5v5: Cover 1 Man, Cover 1 Zone, Cover 2, Cover 3 and Cover 4, each with a rusher 7 yards off the ball. Line him up on either side, play with no rusher (he becomes an extra safety), or add a second rusher. 7v7 plays get six seven-defender looks, including a two-rusher Cover 2 and man with two deep. The scouting report grades your play against every look at once. If the other team lines up differently, drag any defender to match what you see.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. Open Grass runs in any browser on a phone, tablet or computer, with nothing to install.

Can I share plays with my team?

Yes. Send a share link that opens the play and coverage, or save an animated GIF for your team chat.
