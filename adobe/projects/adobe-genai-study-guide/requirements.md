# Requirements

## Business case

Create a durable study resource that can be updated as Adobe GenAI capabilities and documentation evolve.

## Data requirements

- Official source URLs
- Source publication/update dates where available
- Product/version context
- Terminology
- Capability descriptions
- Limitations and prerequisites

## Model evaluation framework

For AI-generated explanations or question-answering:
- factual accuracy against primary sources
- source coverage
- unsupported-claim rate
- clarity for the intended learner
- human review outcome

## Responsible AI guardrails

- Do not fabricate product behavior.
- Flag uncertainty.
- Preserve source provenance.
- Keep a human reviewer in the loop.
- Do not introduce confidential examples.
