# Core Problem-Solving and Strategy Methodologies

## Hypothesis-Driven Approach

Start with an educated hypothesis about the core issue. Test it against evidence. Update or reject the hypothesis based on what the evidence shows.

## MECE

Structure a problem into categories that are mutually exclusive and collectively exhaustive where practical. Use judgment when a strict MECE decomposition would obscure important relationships.

## DMAIC

Define, Measure, Analyze, Improve, Control. Use this when the problem is an operational process that can benefit from a structured improvement cycle.

## SWOT

Summarize internal strengths and weaknesses and external opportunities and threats. Treat SWOT as a framing tool, not as evidence by itself.

## Porter's Five Forces

Use competitive rivalry, supplier power, buyer power, threat of substitutes, and threat of new entrants to structure industry analysis.

## BCG Growth-Share Matrix

Use relative market share and market growth to frame portfolio discussions. Do not present the matrix as a prediction without supporting data.

## McKinsey 7S

Use strategy, structure, systems, shared values, skills, style, and staff to examine organizational alignment.
