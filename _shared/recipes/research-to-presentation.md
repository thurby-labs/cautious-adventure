# Recipe: Research to Presentation

## Steps

1. Complete and validate research first.
2. Define audience and desired outcome.
3. Use `_shared/prompts/presentations/research-to-deck.md`.
4. Reduce the story to the minimum necessary slides.
5. Convert complex relationships into diagrams where useful.
6. Preserve sources and caveats.
7. Review every factual claim against the research artifact.

## Rule

Do not start by designing slides. Start with the decision or message.
