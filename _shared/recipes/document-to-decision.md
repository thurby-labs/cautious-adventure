# Recipe: Document to Decision

## Steps

1. Identify the document's purpose and audience.
2. Extract source-supported claims.
3. Use `_shared/prompts/document-analysis/document-to-brief.md`.
4. Separate source facts from interpretation.
5. Identify missing information.
6. Validate material claims if outside research is permitted.
7. Produce the decision brief.
8. Route unresolved judgment to a human.
