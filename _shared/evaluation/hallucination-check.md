# Hallucination and Unsupported-Claim Check

Run this against an AI-generated artifact before publishing or relying on it.

## Checklist

- [ ] Every material factual claim has a source or an explicit basis.
- [ ] Dates are current enough for the task.
- [ ] Product capabilities are verified when they may have changed.
- [ ] Quotes are traceable to the source.
- [ ] Numbers have a traceable origin.
- [ ] Examples are labeled as examples when they are not real evidence.
- [ ] Inferences are labeled as inferences.
- [ ] Unknowns are not filled with plausible guesses.
- [ ] Conflicting evidence is visible.
- [ ] The final artifact does not imply more certainty than the evidence supports.
