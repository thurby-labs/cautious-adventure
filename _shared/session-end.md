# AI Session End

Copy this into the AI when a substantial work session is ending.

```text
Close this session cleanly.

1. Summarize the work completed.
2. List files created or changed.
3. Record decisions made and why.
4. Record unresolved questions.
5. Record known limitations or risks.
6. Record exact next steps.
7. Identify anything another AI or human must know to continue.
8. Check whether context.md, decisions.md, research.md, next-steps.md, or handoff.md need updates.
9. Review the final diff for accidental private, proprietary, or unrelated content.

Do not claim a task is complete if a required validation step remains undone.
```
