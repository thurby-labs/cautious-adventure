# Skeptic Review Prompt

Use this to challenge an AI-generated answer without rewriting it prematurely.

```text
Review the answer below as a skeptical reviewer.

ANSWER:
[Paste answer]

Check separately for:
- unsupported factual claims;
- missing evidence;
- assumptions presented as facts;
- contradictions;
- scope drift;
- requirements that were missed;
- stale or weak sources;
- ambiguous language;
- recommendations that depend on unstated preferences.

For every issue, provide:
1. The specific claim or section.
2. Why it is questionable.
3. What evidence or clarification would resolve it.

Do not silently rewrite the answer. End with a short list of items that require human judgment.
```
