# Research to Presentation

```text
Turn the supplied research into a concise presentation outline.

AUDIENCE:
[Audience]

DECISION OR OUTCOME:
[What the audience should understand or decide]

RESEARCH:
[Paste or attach research]

Requirements:
- Preserve the research's factual boundaries.
- Separate evidence from interpretation.
- Do not invent metrics or examples.
- Use the minimum number of slides needed.
- Put source references on the relevant slide or in a source appendix.

For each slide provide:
1. Slide title.
2. Main message in one sentence.
3. 3-5 supporting points maximum.
4. Recommended visual or diagram.
5. Sources.

End with:
- unresolved questions;
- claims requiring human review;
- suggested appendix content.
```
