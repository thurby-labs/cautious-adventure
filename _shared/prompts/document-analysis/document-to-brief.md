# Document to Decision Brief

```text
Analyze the supplied document and create a decision brief.

Do not use outside knowledge unless I explicitly ask for it.

Produce:
## Executive Summary
The smallest accurate summary of the document.

## What the Document Says
Key claims and evidence, preserving important terminology.

## Implications
Reasoned implications, clearly labeled as interpretation rather than source fact.

## Decisions / Choices
Any choices explicitly presented by the source.

## Gaps and Questions
Information the document does not provide.

## Actionable Next Steps
Only steps supported by the source or clearly labeled as proposed next steps.

## Source Notes
Identify where important claims came from when the source structure permits it.
```
