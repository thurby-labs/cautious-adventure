# Meeting Notes to Decisions

```text
Convert the meeting material below into a decision record.

SOURCE MATERIAL:
[Paste notes or transcript]

Extract only what the source supports.

Produce:
## Decisions
For each decision: decision, rationale, evidence, owner if explicitly stated, date if explicitly stated.

## Action Items
Action, owner, due date, dependency. Use "Not stated" rather than guessing.

## Open Questions
Question, why it matters, information needed.

## Risks / Concerns
Only include risks actually raised or directly supported by the discussion.

## Follow-up
List the minimum next actions needed to move the work forward.

Do not infer agreement where the discussion shows disagreement or uncertainty.
```
