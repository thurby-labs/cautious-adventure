# Executive Brief Skill

## Purpose

Compress complex material into a decision-useful brief without removing critical caveats.

## Structure

1. Decision or purpose.
2. Executive summary.
3. Key evidence.
4. Implications.
5. Options or decisions, if applicable.
6. Risks and constraints.
7. Next actions.

## Rules

- Lead with the decision or business consequence.
- Prefer concrete nouns and verbs.
- Remove background that does not change understanding.
- Preserve material uncertainty.
- Never manufacture metrics, consensus, or certainty.
