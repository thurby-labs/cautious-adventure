# Research Skill

## Purpose

Turn an ambiguous research question into a source-backed, reusable research artifact.

## Inputs

- question;
- intended decision or use;
- audience;
- time boundary;
- source restrictions;
- required output.

## Method

1. Clarify the question and decision context.
2. Break the question into sub-questions.
3. Identify primary and authoritative sources.
4. Search broadly enough to discover competing evidence.
5. Verify important claims against underlying sources.
6. Record contradictions and gaps.
7. Separate facts, interpretations, assumptions, and unresolved questions.
8. Produce the requested artifact.

## Output

At minimum:
- research question;
- findings;
- evidence/source links;
- uncertainty;
- open questions;
- next steps.

## Quality checks

- No unsupported claims presented as facts.
- Current information is dated when freshness matters.
- Primary sources are preferred.
- Source links are preserved.
- Research can be reused without reopening the original chat.
