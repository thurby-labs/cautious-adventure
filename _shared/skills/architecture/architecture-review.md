# Architecture Review Skill

## Purpose

Evaluate a proposed solution against requirements, constraints, tradeoffs, and operational realities.

## Inputs

- business objective;
- functional requirements;
- non-functional requirements;
- constraints;
- current state;
- proposed architecture;
- assumptions;
- dependencies.

## Review sequence

1. Restate the problem.
2. Verify requirements are testable.
3. Identify missing constraints.
4. Review logical components and boundaries.
5. Review data flows and dependencies.
6. Review security, privacy, resilience, observability, and operations as applicable.
7. Identify tradeoffs.
8. Identify failure modes.
9. Identify open decisions.
10. Produce review findings.

## Finding format

`Severity | Finding | Evidence | Impact | Suggested resolution | Owner/decision needed`

Do not label something a defect when the available information only supports a question or tradeoff.
